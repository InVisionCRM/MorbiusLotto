PLAYER HERO LAB

five replacements for the connected-player block

The block on the live homepage carries a greeting, a VIP sentence, a “while you were away” digest, a resume card and a balance panel — five messages stacked, plus a generic drawn face for an avatar. Each option below cuts the prose down and swaps that face for something that means something: a monogram, a tier medallion, a chip, or a wallet-derived identicon (deterministic — the same wallet always draws the same mark, so it reads as identity, not randomness). Same data in every option, so you're comparing shape, not content. Pick one, or mix — e.g. “option 2 with the medallion from 4”.

most premium

A membership card: identity, tier and balance embossed on one object, with the two things you'd actually click beside it. Text drops from five messages to zero prose — the card states, it doesn't narrate. Avatar becomes a monogram tile.
