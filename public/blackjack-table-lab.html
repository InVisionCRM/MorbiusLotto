What you're looking at

Felt & rail are real GLSL shader materials (procedural weave / wood-grain / leather with a rim+spot lighting rig). Chips and card backs are rasterized on an offscreen canvas, then uploaded as WebGL textures on a chip-stack cylinder and card-back box.

Prototype — Deep-Sea Neon table studio, sibling to slot-builder-lab.html and slot-cabinet-lab.html. Standalone design tool: not wired into the live app. Currency label on chip denominations: MORBIUS.
