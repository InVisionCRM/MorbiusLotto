Your client seed

Verify a round

Every spin draws, in fixed order: 5 reel stops → one multiplier per wild (reel-major) → on free spins the sigil draws (1 count uniform, then 1 cell uniform per sigil) → if the bonus hit, the 24 Night Hunt tile values (row-major) then a Fisher-Yates shuffle permutation (23 uniforms) — all from one stream derived from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bet. Where you aim consumes no randomness: your shot tile index is recorded and the revealed multiplier is re-derived from the committed board and shuffle. Jackpot hits are re-checked from the wild count; pool sizes are account state.
