HOW IT PAYS. Matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left pay — every extra copy on a reel multiplies the ways, up to 4,096. On any win the winning positions LOCK and every other cell respins, again and again while new wins keep landing — a position that keeps paying is the whole SuperStake idea. Sticky wilds hold for three spins. 3+ vault scatters spin the RESTAKE WHEEL (2× to 25× bet). Wins feed your stake level — the ladder above the deck. A random ×2–×10 slam can hit any win.
