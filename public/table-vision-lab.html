COMPOSED TABLE — four directions

All four are built from the same layer kinds — scene, surface, grain, printed markings, rail, seam, trim, stickers, lighting — restacked and restyled. Nothing here is a bitmap; it's all gradients, SVG grain and blend modes, so it's an honest preview of what the layer system can do before any art is commissioned. Pick the direction; palette is one field.

The table sits in a place — this is the "surroundings" half of what you described, and it's the one direction that reads as a venue rather than a graphic. The scene is just two more layers behind the table, so the cost is real but contained. The catch: a tilted surface means seat coordinates no longer map straight to screen space — the existing card pitch control helps, but every stock layout needs re-tuning once.

Each of those is one entry in table.layers[], and each carries a kind, an asset id, a tint, an opacity and a blend mode. Swapping the felt or the rail is one field — that's what makes a library worth having, and what a single painted image can never give you.
