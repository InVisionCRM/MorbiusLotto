Verify a round

Every spin draws 5 reel stops, one multiplier per wild (reel-major), then — on a winning free spin — each chained respin's 5 stops + fresh multipliers for the unfrozen cells, then the 3 claw grabs if the bonus hit — all from one stream derived from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bet. Jackpot hits are re-checked from the wild count; pool sizes are account state.
