Game control

Per-game limits and full oversight — one card per game.
Real: all 22 games and their current min/max come from the live constants in server/src/services/arcade-*.ts. Sample: RTP, wagered, won, winners, biggest win, last play and max-multiplier are placeholders so the layout can be judged at realistic density — no DB access from here. Max able to be won = max bet × top multiplier, against a 50M bankroll; cards turn red when one round could take >10% of it. Multipliers marked ✓ verified were read from the real payout-bounding constants (Dice ~49.5×, Dice x2 ~49×, Limbo 100× via LIMBO_MAX_TARGET_X100, Crash 100× via CRASH_MAX_CASHOUT_X100, Roulette 36×). Ones marked ? unverified have no payout cap constant anywhere in the codebase — their exposure figure here is a guess and the real ceiling is whatever their paytable happens to produce.
