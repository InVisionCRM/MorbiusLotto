Verify a round

Every spin draws, in fixed order: 5 reel stops → one multiplier per wild (reel-major) → on free spins the sigil draws (1 count uniform, then 1 cell uniform per sigil) → the full 12-node Circuit Breach sequence if the bonus hit (2 uniforms per node) — all from one stream derived from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bet. Your ADVANCE/EXTRACT choices are recorded and re-scored against the committed sequence. Jackpot hits are re-checked from the wild count; pool sizes are account state.
