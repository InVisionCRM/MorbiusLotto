How it pays

Drop the ball and it caroms from bumper to bumper — each one it strikes adds its value to your combo multiplier. The ball keeps bouncing until it drains out the bottom, and you\'re paid the full combo. Mellow tables rally longest; Frenzy bounces less but hides a jackpot bumper.
