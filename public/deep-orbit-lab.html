Verify a round

Every spin draws 5 reel stops, one multiplier per rocket wild (reel-major), then one value per Energy Core (reel-major) — all from one stream derived from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bet. When 6+ cores land, the Hold & Spin respins draw per empty socket in row-major order (land, then value). Zero Gravity sticky wilds are derived state, replayed spin-for-spin. Jackpot cores (Mini / Major / full-grid Grand) are re-checked; pool sizes are account state.
