Verify a round

Every spin draws, in fixed order: 5 reel stops → one multiplier per wild (reel-major) → the full 52-card Fisher-Yates Dealer-Duel shuffle if the bonus hit — all from one stream derived from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bet. The duel's ten-plus cards, tie redeals and pot doublings replay deterministically from that deck; the Chip Stack multiplier is derived state recomputed from the spin wins. Jackpot hits are re-checked from the wild count; pool sizes are account state.
