01What you get

Reel Forge is a slot studio in your browser. You design the machine; MORBIUS hosts it, runs the maths, and hands you an <iframe> to paste anywhere.

There are two tiers, and you choose per machine:

TierWhat players useWhat you need
Free playDemo credits, no wallet neededNothing — build and publish
Real moneyAny PRC-20 token you chooseA funded bankroll and 80–99% RTP
No rake. MORBIUS takes no cut of your machine. The house edge you build into the paytable is yours, and it accrues to your bankroll.

02Quick start

The whole path, in the order you'll actually do it.

  1. Open the builder — Reel Forge. Pick a preset from the top-right dropdown so you start from a working machine rather than a blank grid.
  2. Make it yours — grid size, symbols, paytable, bonus round, colours and sound, all in the Control Deck.
  3. Spin it — hit Spin Preview until it feels right, then check Math for the simulated RTP.
  4. Sign in — one click with your wallet, on the Save & Share tab.
  5. Save, then Publish — publishing is what makes the machine reachable outside the builder.
  6. Copy the embed code and paste it into your site.
  7. Optional: go real money — set a token, fund the bankroll, and re-publish with the real-money iframe.

03The studio

Two halves. Everything on the left changes the thing on the right.

Two shortcuts worth knowing straight away:

  • Click the machine itself. Click a symbol, the reels, the frame, the HUD or the background in the preview and the matching editor opens where you clicked.
  • Quick Edit (the row above the deck) jumps straight to the five things people change most: symbols, reels, frame, background, spin button.

04Layout & grid

The shape of the board, and how paylines are drawn on it.

BUILD Layout

Reels (columns) and rows, the win-evaluation mode, and the physical look of the cells — frame style, corner radius, gap, symbol scale, cell background.

Paylines are generated for you from the grid: every row, plus V, inverted-V and zig-zag patterns. Change the grid and the lines regenerate.

BUILD Grid

Grid systems — presets that restructure the board rather than just resize it: classic, scatter-pays grids, megaways, split reels, expanding rows.

05Win flow

What happens after a winning combination lands. This is where a machine stops feeling like a spreadsheet.

  • Cascades (tumbles). Winning symbols shatter, survivors fall, new symbols drop in — and any new win pays again on the same bet.
  • Multiplier ladder. Each cascade in a chain steps the multiplier up, e.g. ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×5 → ×8. Endless keeps the chain going as long as wins keep landing.
  • Respin / locked respin. Winners hold their position and everything else re-spins.
  • Hold & win. Landing symbols lock and grant respins — the classic "collect the board" mechanic.
These multiply your RTP. A ladder that tops out at ×8 on an endless cascade can move a machine from sane to unpayable. After any change here, go to Math and re-run the simulation.

06Symbols & paytable

The symbols are the art and the maths. Every one has a role, a weight and a pay row.

FieldWhat it does
Rolenormal, wild (substitutes) or scatter (pays anywhere / triggers the bonus)
WeightHow often it appears. Higher = more common. This is your main frequency dial.
PaysPayout per match count, as a multiple of the bet unit — the paytable row for that symbol
ArtYour uploaded image (embedded straight into the machine — no image hosting needed)
Size / fitHow the art sits inside its cell
The rule of thumb: high pays need low weights. If you raise a symbol's payout, lower its weight — or you have just raised the RTP of the whole machine.

BUILD Symbol FX

The per-symbol border treatment — neon, shimmer, ember, electric, frost, pulse-ring — with its own colour and speed. Purely cosmetic, and the cheapest way to make premium symbols feel premium.

07Bonus round & meta

The bonus is the thing players chase. The meta is what keeps them on the machine between bonuses.

  • Free spins — a run of spins on the house, usually with a multiplier or extra wilds.
  • Wheel — one spin of a weighted multiplier wheel.
  • Pick game — the player picks boxes to reveal prizes until they hit a collect.

The trigger is your scatter symbol: land enough of them anywhere and the round fires.

Both the bonus and the meta are included in the RTP simulation, so the number on the Math tab already accounts for them.

08Theme, FX, animation & sound

None of this changes the maths. All of it changes whether anyone plays.

Set a big-win threshold you'll actually hit. If the celebration only fires above ×50, most sessions never see it. Around ×8–×15 of the bet keeps the machine feeling alive.

09Math & RTP

Before you publish anything, find out what you actually built.

You get back:

FigureMeansHealthy range
RTPShare of wagers returned to players over the long run92–97% is typical; 80–99% is required to publish a token machine
Hit rateShare of spins that win anything20–35% feels lively without being noisy
Max winBiggest single win seen in the run, as a multiple of betCompare against your win cap
RTP is the house edge, inverted. 94% RTP means the machine keeps 6% of everything wagered, over a long run. Short runs swing wildly in both directions — a funded bankroll is what lets you survive the swings.
Re-run the simulation after any change to symbols, pays, weights, win mode, cascades or the bonus. Art and sound changes can't move it.

10Wallet sign-in

Saving to the community needs an identity. That identity is your wallet — and it matters more than it looks.

What actually happens

  1. Your wallet is asked which account to use. If it has already connected to MORBIUS, it reuses that account and skips straight to the next step.
  2. You sign a message. Plain text, no transaction, no gas, and it cannot move funds — it only proves you hold the key.
  3. The server checks the signature and gives you a session. That's the sign-in.
The account you sign in with owns the machine. It is also the only address a bankroll withdrawal can pay out to. If your wallet auto-connected with the wrong account, click Use a different account before you save anything.
No wallet in this browser? The builder talks to a wallet that injects itself into the page — a browser extension, or your wallet app's built-in browser. Open the builder inside that browser and sign in there.

12Embed it on your site

One tag. No SDK, no build step, no account for your visitors.

Free play — anyone can play instantly, no wallet:

<iframe src="https://morbius.io/embed/YOUR-SLUG"
        width="480" height="720"
        style="border:0;" allow="autoplay"></iframe>

Real money — the same machine, playing for your token:

<iframe src="https://morbius.io/embed/YOUR-SLUG?play=real"
        width="480" height="780"
        style="border:0;" allow="autoplay"></iframe>

Sizing it

The cabinet is built tall and narrow. 480×720 suits free play; the real-money version needs roughly 60px more height for the wallet bar. To make it fluid, wrap it:

<div style="max-width:480px;margin:0 auto">
  <iframe src="https://morbius.io/embed/YOUR-SLUG"
          width="100%" height="720"
          style="border:0;display:block" allow="autoplay"></iframe>
</div>
It works on any domain. The embed page sets its framing policy to allow all sites, so you don't need to register your domain with anyone.
A note on browser storage. In a third-party iframe some browsers isolate storage per-site, so a free-play demo balance may not persist between visits the way it does on morbius.io itself. Real-money balances are held server-side against your wallet, so they are unaffected.

13Pick your betting token

Optional, and only worth doing once the machine plays well. Any PRC-20 on PulseChain can be the currency.

Credits vs tokens

Spins are always counted in whole credits — that's what keeps the maths exact. The credit value is the bridge to your token:

  • Default: 1 credit = 0.001 token.
  • Deposits convert token → credits, rounding down.
  • Cash-outs convert credits → token at the same rate.

Pick a credit value that makes your bet ladder feel sane. If a token is worth a lot, make each credit smaller.

The token locks on the first deposit. Once real value is in the pool, the machine's token cannot be swapped — the escrow pool is bound to it. Choose deliberately.
Fee-on-transfer tokens work, with a caveat. They're allowed, but the machine detects the shortfall and carries a permanent warning badge, because less arrives than you sent.

14Fund the bankroll

The bankroll is the house money. It pays your winners, and it collects your edge.

Funding, step by step

  1. Type an amount in the bankroll box and hit Fund bankroll.
  2. Approve — your wallet asks permission for the escrow to move that much of your token. (Transaction 1.)
  3. Fund — the tokens move into this machine's escrow pool. (Transaction 2.)
  4. Credit — the server verifies the deposit on-chain before crediting your bankroll. The panel walks you through all three stages.
Where the money sits. In the Tournament Prize Escrow contract, in a pool keyed to your machine — the same escrow the poker tournaments use. Not in a hot wallet, and not mixed with other machines.

Getting it back out

Withdraw sends bankroll back to the owner address. Two things worth knowing:

  • Withdrawals still work on a disabled machine — turning a machine off never traps your funds.
  • Player balances are a liability against the bankroll. Leave enough to cover what players are holding, or they can't cash out.
Fund it properly or it won't run. Max bet is derived from the bankroll (see below), so a thin bankroll means a machine that pauses itself constantly.

15How betting works

You don't set bet limits by hand. They're derived, so a machine can never promise more than it can pay.

The player's journey

  1. Connect & sign in — same one-click flow you used.
  2. Deposit — tokens go to the machine's escrow and become credits.
  3. Play — every spin is settled server-side against their credit balance.
  4. Cash out — credits convert back to tokens and are sent to their wallet.
Bets never leave the player's wallet spin by spin. They deposit once, play from a balance, and cash out when they're done — one signature, not one per spin.

The limits, and where they come from

LimitHow it's set
Min betDerived from your paytable so the smallest bet still pays whole credits
Max betbankroll ÷ (10 × win cap) — it floats with your bankroll
Win capBiggest payout as a multiple of bet. Default 5000×. Every round is clamped to it.
Auto-pauseIf the bankroll can't cover the minimum bet, the machine pauses itself

So: fund more, and players can bet bigger. Let it run down, and the machine protects itself before it can promise a payout it can't make.

The 80–99% RTP gate. A machine with a token configured will not publish outside that band. Below 80% is a machine nobody should play; above 99% is one that will drain your bankroll. Free-play machines are only warned, not blocked.

16Provably fair

Every real spin is settled on the server, and every spin can be checked afterwards.

  • Each spin draws from a server seed (committed in advance as a hash), your player's client seed, and an incrementing nonce.
  • The server seed is published when it rotates — so nobody, including you, can change a result after the fact.
  • Anyone can replay a spin from the revealed seed and confirm the cards fell as claimed.
  • Spins record the machine version they ran on, so a later edit can't rewrite history.
This is also your protection as an operator: a player claiming a rigged result can be answered with maths rather than an argument.

17Read your stats

Once a machine is saved, the stats panel appears under the community controls.

FigureWhat to do with it
Spins / playersIs anyone actually playing? Traffic problems look nothing like maths problems.
Wagered vs paid outThe gap is your gross edge over that period
Live RTPWhat players really got. Compare with the simulated RTP.
Best winYour worst day so far, from the bankroll's point of view
Bankroll vs player balancesWhat you hold vs what you owe. Bankroll should comfortably exceed it.
Daily bars14 days of activity — did that change help or hurt?
Live RTP only means something with volume. Over a few hundred spins it swings wildly. Over tens of thousands it should converge toward your simulated figure — and if it doesn't, something is wrong with the machine, not with luck.

18Make changes to a live machine

Machines are versioned, so editing a published one is safe and reversible in the ways that matter.

  1. Edit as normal in the builder.
  2. Save to Community — this writes a new version and returns the machine to draft. Your embed keeps serving the last published version in the meantime.
  3. Re-check Math if you touched anything that moves the RTP.
  4. Publish again to push it live.
Old spins keep their old maths. Each spin records the version it ran on, so history stays verifiable after you change the machine.
Token machines re-face the RTP gate on every publish. If an edit pushes RTP outside 80–99%, publish is refused until you bring it back — the old version stays live, so players are never left on a broken machine.

Turning a machine off

Disabling stops new play. Players can still cash out their balances, and you can still withdraw your bankroll — nothing gets stranded.

19Troubleshooting

Sign-in doesn't finish

The status line names the step it's waiting on. If it says it's waiting on your wallet, open the wallet — a request is usually sitting there unanswered. If a wallet request is already open from an earlier click, finish or dismiss that one first.

"No wallet detected in this browser"

The builder needs a wallet that injects itself into the page. Use a browser extension wallet, or open the page in your wallet app's own browser.

Publish is refused on RTP

Your simulated RTP is outside 80–99%. Adjust pays or weights — usually the top-paying symbol's weight — and re-run the Math tab.

"Get embed code" is greyed out

The machine isn't published yet. Save to Community, then Publish.

The machine says it's paused

The bankroll can no longer cover the minimum bet. Fund it and play resumes automatically.

Max bet is lower than I set

Max bet is derived from the bankroll, not chosen. Fund more and it rises on its own.

Players can't cash out

Player balances are paid from the bankroll. If you withdrew too much, top it back up.
