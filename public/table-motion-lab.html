Table studio motion — before / after

The left column is what the studio ships today. The right is motion taken from the table itself rather than from a UI kit — a card dealt, a chip set on felt, a chip spun on its edge, a stack built. Click Replay on any row; hover the right-hand tile for the lamp sweep.

1 · Changing step

Before: an 8px fade-up — the same transition as a cookie banner. After: the panel arrives like a card off the shoe: in from the dealer's side, turned, landing flat with the overshoot a card has when it slides across cloth and stops. The perspective is real, so it reads as having travelled rather than having scaled.
