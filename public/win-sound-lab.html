▶ Win Sound Lab

Thirty CC0 samples, licence-checked one page at a time, stacked into a win sting you can tune. Pick a layer, drag its level, hit Play tier, repeat until it sounds like money.

Your ears decide this one. These files were chosen from duration, attack and measured tonal balance — nobody has listened to them yet. The defaults are an educated guess, not a recommendation. If a layer sounds cheap or wrong, swap it; that is what this page is for.

The recipe

Each tier stacks up to four layers. Delay is how long after the win a layer starts — staggering the tail behind the body is most of what makes a sting sound produced rather than piled up. Set a layer to none to drop it; ◉ solos one. Levels are already loudness-matched per file, so swapping a sample keeps roughly the balance you dialled in.
