HOW IT PAYS. Groups of 5+ matching symbols touching orthogonally pay, anywhere on the board. Winners are cleared and the board cascades; every clear in a chain steps the multiplier ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×5 → ×8. The Green Wick wild joins any cluster and walks one reel left every spin until he leaves the board. Markers (the blood-oath coins) collect above the reels — a full set of 15 pays 20× bet. 3+ markers in one spin trigger CONTRACT SPINS. A random ×2–×10 slam can hit any win.
