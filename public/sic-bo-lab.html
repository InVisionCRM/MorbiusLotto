Prototype — Deep-Sea Neon style, single-player on the arcade2 pattern (like Greed Dice / Dragon Tiger / Mines).
The three dice here come from an in-page seeded RNG for feel; the real build draws them from the server provably-fair HMAC stream (/api/arcade/sic-bo/*) with the classic Macau paytable. Every roll re-derives from the seed in Verify. Currency: MORBIUS chips.

Your client seed

Verify a round

All three dice are drawn from a server seed committed (hashed) before your bets — the seed is revealed once the round settles, and every zone re-settles from the same faces.
