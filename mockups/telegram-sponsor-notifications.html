How the broadcast works:
  • Track group membership in a new table — telegram_bot_groups (chat_id, title, added_at, removed_at) — populated by the bot's my_chat_member webhook so it auto-updates whenever an admin adds or removes the bot.
  • On successful purchaseTableLogoSponsorship, fan out the same payload to every row in that table with removed_at IS NULL.
  • Store each posted message in telegram_sponsor_cards (sponsorship_id, chat_id, message_id) so when the slot gets trumped or expires the bot can editMessageText in every group to swap the "Trump for X" button to "Slot open · be the next sponsor" or grey it out as expired.
Burn implementation note: the current purchaseTableLogoSponsorship at server/src/services/poker-game.service.ts:2029 deducts MORBIUS from the buyer's players.balance without crediting it anywhere — already effectively "burnt" in off-chain accounting. If you want a real on-chain burn (visible on the explorer), that's a separate ticket: route the wei to 0x0…dEaD at the contract layer.
