How to play
How it works:
  • Five tabs always visible at the top of the sidebar. Whatever you click is what fills the rest of the panel — nothing else to look at.
  • Activity is the default tab on session open (preserves current behaviour). Chat input only appears on this tab.
  • Sponsor tab is the same Pulse Beacon design as the intermission ad, but persistent — players and spectators interact with the sponsor mechanic here instead of in a modal.
  • Action UI is unaffected. The Fold / Call / Raise buttons live on the table itself (PokerActions.tsx), not in the sidebar. You can sit on Stats forever and still act the moment the table prompts you.
  • Urgency cue is the table's existing yellow "Your turn" pulse — already there, already loud. The sidebar doesn't need its own attention badge.
