# Keno on MORBlotto — How It Actually Works

Use this for **Crypto Keno** / **MORBlotto** voice scripts. Much generic Keno copy (live lounge draws, Club Keno dollar paytables, “25–35% house edge”) does **not** apply.

---

## PART 1: How the game works on MORBlotto

### Setup (matches `CryptoKeno.sol`)

- **Board:** Numbers **1–80** (standard Keno field).
- **Player picks:** **Spot size from 1 to 10** on the default paytable path the UI and contract ship with (the contract allows a higher configured `maxSpot` in principle, but default initialization and app paytables are built around **1–10** spots).
- **Draw:** **20 distinct winning numbers** are drawn from 80 for each play.
- **Match count (“hits”):** How many of your picks appear in those 20 numbers determines the **gross** payout multiplier from the paytable.

### Instant (on-chain) play

- **Quick / instant Keno** is a **single transaction**: pick numbers, wager, draw, and payout settle atomically on-chain. It is **not** the same as a live lounge game where draws happen every five minutes.
- **Minimum wager:** **1 MORBIUS** (1e18 smallest units) unless the deployment changes constants.
- **Maximum gross payout cap:** Contract enforces a **maximum gross payout per ticket** (designed around **100k × 25× = 2.5M MORBIUS** scale at 18 decimals).

### Fees

- **5% of the gross payout** is taken on payout and routed per configuration (MORBIUS distribution, burn, platform, LP) — analogous to Plinko’s payout fee model. Narrate **net** winnings when talking about what the player receives after fees.

## PART 3: Odds and “strategy”

- **Pure chance:** No pick pattern changes the draw; the chain uses deterministic randomness / provably fair machinery as implemented — do not imply lucky numbers or hot/cold stats change odds.
- **Spot choice** only changes **volatility and paytable shape**, not a secret edge from “lucky” picks.
- Generic claims like “live Keno has 25–35% house edge” or “online Keno is always 1–5%” are **not** authoritative for this product; cite **paytable + 5% payout fee** instead.

---

## PART 4: Player care (still valid)

- **Speed:** Instant Keno can be played very quickly — bet sizing matters.
- **Bankroll rule of thumb:** Use small fractions of bankroll per ticket so long dry spells do not wipe the session.
- **Responsible play:** Negative-expectation entertainment; only wager what you can afford to lose.
