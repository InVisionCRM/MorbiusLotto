## 📖 Introduction

**Richard Heart** (born Richard James Schueler) is one of the most polarizing, successful, and heavily debated figures in the cryptocurrency industry. As the founder of **HEX**, **PulseChain**, and **PulseX**, he has built a multi-billion dollar ecosystem backed by a fiercely loyal community known as "Hexicans."

Heart is widely known for his flamboyant "outrage marketing," his absolute refusal to rely on middlemen, and his profound (and sometimes brutal) philosophical views on time, health, and wealth creation.

## 🧠 Core Beliefs & Philosophy

Richard Heart’s approach to business and life is a mix of extreme pragmatism, evolutionary biology, and unapologetic capitalism. 

### 1. Eradicating Middlemen & Gatekeepers
Heart believes the original purpose of cryptocurrency was entirely lost when people started leaving their coins on centralized exchanges (like FTX, Celsius, or BlockFi). His core ethos is **"Not your keys, not your coins."** He builds smart contracts with zero admin keys to ensure that no CEO, board of directors, or middleman can ever stop a user from accessing their own yield or funds.

### 2. "Outrage Marketing" (The Flex)
Heart is infamous for wearing million-dollar watches, buying the world's largest cut black diamond (The Enigma), and flaunting designer clothes. However, he claims this is a calculated strategy: **Outrage Marketing**. He believes that in a world of infinite scrolling, "you gotta scream real loud to punch through the noise." The wealth signaling is designed to make people angry or curious enough to look at his actual products.

### 3. Delaying Gratification
The entire mechanism of HEX is built on Heart's belief that human beings are terrible at delaying gratification. He designed his crypto ecosystem to financially penalize the impatient and reward the patient. He believes the ultimate cheat code to wealth in any market is simply locking away capital and waiting.

### 4. *SciVive* & Life Extension
Outside of finance, Heart authored a free self-help book called ***SciVive*** (Science + Survival). It focuses on extreme life optimization, life extension technologies, and avoiding biological traps. He heavily advocates against gambling, drinking, and wasting time on video games, urging his followers to fund anti-aging research (such as the SENS Research Foundation, to which the PulseChain community donated millions).

## ⚙️ The Ecosystem

Richard Heart's legacy in blockchain is defined by three interconnected protocols, all built to run autonomously without his input.

1. **HEX (The CD):** A blockchain Certificate of Deposit that pays inflation to users who lock their tokens up, rather than paying miners.
2. **PulseChain (The Network):** A Layer-1 hard fork of Ethereum built to lower gas fees, which famously airdropped a 1:1 copy of every Ethereum token to its users.
3. **PulseX (The DEX):** The primary decentralized exchange on PulseChain, featuring a deflationary buy-and-burn mechanism.

## 🏛️ The SEC Lawsuit & 2025 Legal Victory

Heart's controversial marketing eventually drew the attention of the U.S. government. In **July 2023**, the SEC sued Richard Heart and his projects, accusing him of selling unregistered securities and misappropriating funds to buy luxury goods.

However, in a landmark moment for the crypto industry in **early 2025**, a U.S. District Judge **completely dismissed the SEC's lawsuit**. The court ruled that the SEC failed to establish jurisdiction over Heart (who resides in Europe) and dismissed the fraud claims in full. Heart declared he had "defeated the SEC completely," an event that cemented his legendary status among his supporters as a founder who fought the U.S. government and won.
