# The Ultimate Guide to Blackjack: Rules and Strategy

Blackjack is the most popular casino card game in the world because it combines simple mechanics with a deep mathematical foundation. This guide will take you from the basic rules to the "Basic Strategy" required to minimize the house edge.

---

## Morbius — rules that differ from generic casino copy

- **Dealer rule:** The dealer **hits soft 17** (a hand totaling 17 that still counts as soft because of an ace). Do **not** say “dealer stands on all 17s” for Morbius.
- **Player actions implemented:** **Hit, stand, double down, split.** Ten-value cards of different ranks (e.g. king + jack) can be **split** when our rules treat them as a pair of tens.
- **Not offered in server logic:** **Insurance** and **surrender** are **not** part of the documented Morbius blackjack action set — avoid telling players to take insurance or surrender here.
- **Fairness:** Games use a **server-side shoe / deck** with **provably fair** seeding (hashed server seed, client seed, nonce) so outcomes can be verified — different from a physical shoe, but not “live dealer peek” unless the UI explicitly shows it.
- **Modes:** **Single-player** blackjack and **multiplayer / tournament** blackjack share these dealer rules where the same service logic applies.

The basic-strategy charts below are still useful, but charts tuned for **dealer stands on soft 17** differ slightly; mentally favor **more aggressive player draws** against a dealer ace or when the chart assumes S17.

## 1. The Goal of the Game
Your goal is to beat the dealer’s hand without going over 21. If your hand exceeds 21, you "bust" and lose your bet immediately.

### The Card Values:
* **Numbered Cards (2-10):** Worth their face value.
* **Face Cards (J, Q, K):** Worth 10.
* **Aces:** Worth either 1 or 11 (whichever benefits your hand more).

---

## 2. The Rules of Play
1. **The Deal:** You are dealt two cards face-up. The dealer gets one card face-up and one face-down (the "hole card").
2. **The Options (Morbius):**
    * **Hit:** Take another card.
    * **Stand:** Keep your current hand.
    * **Double Down:** Double your original bet in exchange for exactly one more card.
    * **Split:** If you have a splittable pair (including two ten-value cards), split into two hands (requires an additional bet).
    * **Surrender / Insurance:** Common in live casinos; **not** offered in Morbius’s implemented blackjack flows.
3. **Dealer Rules (Morbius):** The dealer **hits** on **soft 17** and stands on **hard 17+** (and on **soft** hands that are no longer soft after hitting, e.g. ace counted as 1).

## 4. Golden Rules for Beginners
1. **Insurance:** In live casinos it is usually a poor bet — on **Morbius** it is **not** a listed player action; do not instruct users to “decline insurance” as if a button exists unless the UI adds it later.
2. **Learn the "Hard 16" rule:** If you have 16 and the dealer shows 2 through 6, you stand. If the dealer shows 7 through Ace, you hit.
3. **Always Split Aces and 8s:** A pair of 8s is a 16 (the worst hand in Blackjack); splitting them gives you a much better chance of landing two 18s.
4. **Bankroll Management:** Never bet more than 5% of your total session bankroll on a single hand.

***Disclaimer:** Blackjack is a game of chance. Basic strategy reduces the house edge but, with **dealer hits soft 17**, the edge is slightly higher than in S17 games. On Morbius, play responsibly and follow the in-app rules and limits.*
